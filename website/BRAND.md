# WarraqCraft brand identity

## Name story

**Warraq** (ورّاق / *warrāq*) — the papermaker, stationer and book-copier of
the medieval Islamic world; the person who turned ink and paper into books
before print existed. **Craft** — the working toolchain. Together: *the
warraq's desk, rebuilt in Rust.*

## Tagline

**Write. Typeset. Keep it yours.**

Short forms: "local-first LaTeX" · "the typesetter's desk, rebuilt in Rust".

## Voice

Direct, craftsmanlike, a little bookish. Honest about limits ("LaTeX-compatible",
never "is TeX"). No hype adjectives without a fact attached.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#F6F1E7` | page background, light surfaces |
| Paper deep | `#EFE6D4` | strips, table headers, footer |
| Ink | `#17233B` | primary text, dark sections, logo field |
| Ink soft | `#3D4A66` | secondary text |
| Illumination gold | `#C99A2C` | primary accent, CTAs, logo details |
| Gold deep | `#A87F1E` | accent text on light, hover depth |
| Seal red | `#B84A39` | secondary accent, warnings |
| Sage teal | `#2E7D6B` | success, "verified", kickers |

Manuscript logic: paper is warm, ink is deep navy (not pure black), gold is
the illuminator's metal, red is the binder's seal.

## Typography

- **Display / headings:** Charter → Iowan Old Style → Georgia → serif.
  Serif is the product's heritage; never use a geometric sans for headings.
- **Body / UI:** system sans (`system-ui`, Segoe UI, Noto Sans).
- **Code / data:** ui-monospace / Cascadia Mono / Consolas.
- **Arabic display:** Noto Naskh Arabic or Amiri before serif fallback.

## Logo

`logo.svg` — a geometric pen nib rising over a page-corner, ink field with
paper-white nib and gold breather-hole/accent. Original artwork; use at
≥24 px; clear space ≥ ¼ of width. Don't recolor the ink field.

## Wordmark

"WarraqCraft" set in the display serif, "Warraq" ink, "Craft" gold-deep.
Never "Warraqcraft", "WaraqCraft", or "Warraq Craft" in running text
except in prose discussion.

## Don'ts

- Don't say "TeX editor" — always "LaTeX-compatible".
- Don't imply cloud/sync/accounts; local-first is the brand promise.
- Don't borrow iconography from other TeX editors (clean-room rule applies
  to brand too).
